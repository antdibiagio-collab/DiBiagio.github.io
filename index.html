About me

I'm a senior at the University of New Haven, graduating in May 2027 with a Bachelor of Science in Marketing. Since 2022, I've worked as an Operations Manager in the pharmacy at CVS, where I lead a team of more than six associates and use customer feedback to improve service and keep customers coming back. Balancing a leadership role with a full course load has taught me to manage my time, stay calm under pressure, and communicate clearly with people from all walks of life.

I chose marketing because I enjoy how it connects people. At its best, it helps a brand understand what someone needs and gives them a reason to trust it. I'm especially drawn to digital media, market research, brand strategy, advertising, and customer behavior.

Outside the classroom, I've won first place in both the BUSA 100 Pitch Competition and the university's Hult Prize OnCampus competition, consulted for an on-campus organization, and written for The Charger Bulletin. I'm also HubSpot certified in Inbound Marketing and Sales Software. I'm looking for a role, or an MBA program, where I can keep learning while contributing right away. In my free time I go to the cinema, read, and write, and I think those habits have made me a better storyteller.

Projects

AegisPack: From Classroom Pitch to Regional Stage

Hult Prize OnCampus winner (advanced to regionals)

My team of five developed a business concept for an everyday-looking backpack with a ballistic panel, aimed at students and families concerned about safety. I built the marketing plan, developed the financials, and presented to the judges. The marketing plan combined Instagram and TikTok lifestyle content, Facebook ads for parents, Google search ads, and a short-film concept. To back it up, we surveyed 100+ people, sized the market, compared competitors, and modeled five years of revenue and costs.

Tools: PowerPoint, Excel

What I learned: A sensitive product calls for careful messaging, and customer research should shape the tone of every message. Building the financials also taught me to test whether a marketing idea can pay for itself.

Why it shows my potential: It combines creativity, research, and numbers in one project. I tested the idea with real people, planned how to reach them, and defended the plan in front of judges, which is what marketing work looks like.
